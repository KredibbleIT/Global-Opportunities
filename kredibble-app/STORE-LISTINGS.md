**Full description** (limit 4,000 chars — used: ~1,280; same copy on both stores)

Global Opportunity Desk helps ambitious people find real opportunities and helps organisations find the right talent, all in one app.

FOR OPPORTUNITY SEEKERS
- Browse jobs, internships, scholarships, fellowships, grants and events from trusted organisations.
- Save the opportunities you like and track your applications in one place.
- Build your professional profile so employers can find you.
- Read free career guides on writing a CV, preparing for interviews and winning scholarships.
- Stay up to date with the latest career and scholarship news.
- Join community channels to learn from others and hear about new openings first.
- Meet the ambassadors, team and partners behind the platform.
- Ask the smart assistant your career questions.

FOR EMPLOYERS AND ORGANISATIONS
- Post jobs, internships, grants and events in minutes.
- Search the talent directory by skills and role.
- Review applicants and move them through your hiring pipeline.
- Build a verified company profile that candidates can trust.
- Start your own community channel and share updates with followers.

WHY Global Opportunity Desk
- One place for opportunities across Africa and beyond.
- Vetted listings from trusted organisations.
- Simple, fast and free to get started.

Download Global Opportunity Desk today and take your next step.
