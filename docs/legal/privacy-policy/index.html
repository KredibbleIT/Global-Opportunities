Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 Controller: Kredibble IT (Global Opportunities Desk) Contact: privacy@globalopportunitydesk.com

1. Information we collect

We collect personal data you provide when you use the Kredibble platform:

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, and password hash.
  • Profile data: CV/resume, skills, education, work experience, and career preferences.
  • Application data: job, internship, and grant applications you submit.
  • Verification data: company documents for hirer/company verification.
  • Technical data: IP address, device information, and push notification tokens.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To match you with relevant opportunities.
  • To process applications you submit.
  • To verify your credentials and company documents.
  • To send verification codes and password-reset emails.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the platform.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service providers (email delivery, cloud hosting, analytics, error tracking).
  • Employers and grantmakers when you apply to their opportunities (your application data).
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

4. Data retention

  • Rejected applicant CVs: redacted after 180 days.
  • Rejected company verification documents: purged after 90 days.
  • Account deletion tombstones: retained 7 years (anti-fraud).
  • Audit logs: retained 1 year.

5. Your rights

You may:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Delete your account using the "Delete My Account" option in the app, or by emailing privacy@globalopportunitydesk.com.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

6. Security

We protect your data with:

  • bcrypt (cost 12) for password hashing.
  • TLS encryption in transit.
    • Encrypted at-rest backups.
  • Role-based access controls and audit logging.

7. International transfers

Your data may be processed on cloud platforms located outside Ghana that provide equivalent protection.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
